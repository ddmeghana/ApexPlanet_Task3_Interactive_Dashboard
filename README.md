# 📌 Core KPIs Defined

## 1. Total Sales
**Formula:** `SUM(Sales)`

Measures total revenue generated by the business.

---

## 2. Total Profit
**Formula:** `SUM(Profit)`

Represents overall business profitability.

---

## 3. Profit Margin
**Formula:** `(Profit / Sales) × 100`

Measures how efficiently sales are converted into profit.

---

## 4. Average Order Value (AOV)
**Formula:** `Total Sales / Total Orders`

Measures average customer spending per order.

---

## 5. Total Orders
**Formula:** `COUNTD(Order_ID)`

Measures the total number of unique customer orders.

# 📈 Key Insights

- The Consumer segment contributes the highest share of orders and sales.
- Technology category generates the highest profitability.
- High discounts negatively impact profit margins.
- Western and Eastern regions contribute strong sales performance.
- Monthly sales trends indicate consistent business growth over time.

---

# 💡 Business Recommendations

- Focus on high-performing customer segments for targeted marketing.
- Optimize discount strategies to improve profitability.
- Expand operations in strong-performing regions.
- Improve underperforming categories through pricing and promotional strategies.
