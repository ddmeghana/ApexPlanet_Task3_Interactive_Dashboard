# Deep-Dive Analysis Report – Superstore Dataset

## Objective
The objective of this analysis is to evaluate business performance using core KPIs and perform segmentation analysis to understand how customer segments, regions, categories, and discounts influence sales and profitability.

## Core KPIs
1. Total Sales = SUM(Sales)
2. Total Profit = SUM(Profit)
3. Profit Margin = Total Profit / Total Sales × 100
4. Average Order Value = Total Sales / Number of Orders
5. Average Discount = AVG(Discount)

## Deep-Dive Area
Segmentation Analysis was selected because the dataset contains customer, region, product, and discount dimensions that can help identify profitable and underperforming business groups.

## Key Findings
- Sales and profit vary significantly across customer segments.
- Regional performance differs across West, East, Central, and South.
- Technology shows strong profitability compared to other categories.
- Higher discounts reduce profit margin in several cases.
- Profitability should be evaluated along with sales, not sales alone.

## Business Recommendations
- Focus on high-profit categories such as Technology.
- Review discount strategy, especially where high discounts reduce profit.
- Improve performance in weaker regions through targeted campaigns.
- Retain high-value customer segments using personalized offers.
